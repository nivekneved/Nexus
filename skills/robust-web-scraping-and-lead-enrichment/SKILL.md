# Robust Web Scraping & Lead Enrichment Skill

## Overview
This skill governs high-success-rate data gathering, resilient scraping, and lead verification for autonomous research agents.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Stealth & Anti-Bot Bypassing**:
   - Rotating User-Agents, TLS fingerprint masking, and headless browser navigation.
   - Fallback cascading across requests, Scrapling, and Playwright engines.
2. **Deliverability & MX Verification**:
   - Direct SMTP socket handshakes to verify mailboxes before sending outreach.
   - Domain existence checks and DNS MX record validation to prevent hard bounces.
3. **Structured Lead Enrichment**:
   - Pydantic schema validation for extracted contact records (Name, Role, Company, Email, Phone, Niche).
   - Automated deduplication and suppression list checking.
