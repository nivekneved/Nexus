For more than two excruciating years, while other children were running and laughing, 8-year-old Alison suffered from severe, unexplained pain. After multiple hospital admissions and grueling examinations, doctors finally confirmed her heartbreaking diagnosis: Hirschsprung Disease.

“Hirschsprung disease is a rare congenital condition where nerve cells fail to form in parts of the intestine. Without urgent surgical reconstruction, it leads to severe toxic intestinal blockages, unbearable chronic pain, and fatal complications.”

Due to the critical severity of her condition, specialist pediatric surgeons ordered urgent surgical intervention. Thanks to initial compassionate donations, Alison underwent the primary surgery.

However, her battle is far from over. Alison now requires intensive post-operative recovery care, specialized medications, clinical follow-ups, and rehabilitation supplies to heal safely. Her humble family cannot shoulder these remaining hospital costs alone.
